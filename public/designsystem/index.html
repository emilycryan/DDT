Components

Buttons

A 44 px minimum target, 0.5 rem radius, and a subtle lift on hover. Focus shows a golden-yellow ring for visibility.

Primary
Navy fill, white label. Hover deepens to --primary-darker and lifts 1 px.
Secondary
Navy outline that fills on hover. Used for lower-emphasis actions.
Accent
Golden yellow with dark label, reserved for the single most important call to action.
Components

Cards

Rounded 0.75 rem containers with soft elevation. Featured cards invert to the navy gradient; quotes use a hard left rule.

Standard card

White surface, light-grey hairline border, and a layered shadow that deepens on hover.

Featured card

Navy-to-blue gradient with white type. Used to spotlight a primary pathway or program.

"I cut my risk in half with small weekly changes — and a program that fit my life."
